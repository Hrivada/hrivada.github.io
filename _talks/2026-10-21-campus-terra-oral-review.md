**Authors:** H. Rivada-Rodríguez, J. Rodríguez, A. Conde-Fernández

Oral communication (session OC 1) summarising the systematic review on polymeric materials for 3D-printed orthopaedic devices: polymer families used in material extrusion, how process parameters govern mechanical anisotropy, validation from coupons to devices, and a minimum dataset that every study should report.
