*3D-printing polymers for external veterinary orthoses and prostheses: an analysis of the clinical evidence.*

Literature review on the use of FDM 3D-printing polymers in veterinary orthopaedics. PLA is used for main structures, PETG for areas with higher mechanical or thermal demands, and TPU for sockets and cushioning. Clinical applications cover dogs, cats, cattle and wild and domestic birds, mainly through socket prostheses, external orthoses and modular mobility aids.
