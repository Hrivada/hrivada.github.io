*[Versión en español](/es/)*

I am a PhD student at the University of Santiago de Compostela (Campus Terra, Lugo), in the Doctoral Programme in Engineering for Rural and Civil Development. I am a member of the BioMODEM research group (GI-1720) and work with the AMGeo group and the iTERRA institute.

My research develops a low-cost workflow to design, optimise and validate personalised veterinary orthopaedic devices made by FDM 3D printing.

## Research interests

- High-speed FDM printing and mechanical characterisation of polymers (PLA, PETG, TPU)
- Finite element analysis and topology optimisation of orthopaedic devices
- Custom prostheses and orthoses for animals: design, fabrication and validation
- 3D scanning and digital workflows from patient to printed device

## Collaboration

I am open to collaborations with veterinary clinics, rehabilitation centres and wildlife recovery centres interested in custom 3D-printed orthopaedic devices. Feel free to [get in touch](mailto:hector.rivada@rai.usc.es).
