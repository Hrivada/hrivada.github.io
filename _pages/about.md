*[Versión en español](/es/)*

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Santiago de Compostela (Campus Terra, Lugo), in the Doctoral Programme in Engineering for Rural and Civil Development. I am a member of the BioMODEM research group (GI-1720) and work with the AMGeo group and the iTERRA institute.

My research focuses on low-cost FDM 3D printing for personalised veterinary orthopaedics: material characterisation, finite element modelling and topology optimisation, and the fabrication and validation of custom prostheses and orthoses.
